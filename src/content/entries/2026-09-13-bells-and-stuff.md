A short sound and image study built around a floating particle cloud. The sound pushes and brightens the particles, while small areas of blur appear briefly and then clear. Most of the image stays sharp.

The visual follows a limited set of rules: one cloud, one palette, a fixed camera, repeated reactions, and gradual settling between events.
