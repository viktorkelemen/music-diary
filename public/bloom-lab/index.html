About this sound & the hardware controls

The first six chords reproduce the reel's notes. Each of the four exploration chord families has open or character, close, wide, and low voicings. The Quartal group stacks perfect fourths: C–F–B♭, C–F–B♭–E♭, or C–F–B♭–E♭–A♭, with wide and low versions of the four-note chord. These open, suspended chords work well for evolving drones; try the Quartal drift starting point. Transpose moves the whole stack to another root. At full just intonation, stacked fourths use pure 4:3 ratios; wide and low voicings preserve those tuning relationships across octaves. They use the same five-voice engine and have their own just-intonation targets. Original reel mode starts these new chords with pure sine notes; Bloom adds moving harmonics. Choose Sine, Triangle, Saw, or Square for another base waveform. Set Bloom amount to zero to hear the base wave alone. The Harmonic count control sets the bloom's range; the base wave uses up to 24 harmonics, with high harmonics removed as pitch rises. Original overtones applies only to reel chords in Original reel mode. Waveforms share the same fundamental level, so brighter shapes may sound louder.

The chord popup groups all 29 voicings by family, with search and base-register note previews. Tension adds C–F♯–B (a tritone and a perfect fourth) and C–F♯–C (two equal tritones). In full just intonation, the first uses a pure upper fourth; the double tritone keeps its symmetric octave division. Stillness, Expansion, and Tension offer contrasting starting points. Inner motion holds three voices of the four-note quartal chord while the fourth follows a slow melody. Each voice has an independent note shift and an optional four-step loop, with step offsets measured in equal-tempered semitones relative to its shifted, tuned note. All enabled melodies share the step length; stopped playback pauses their clock.

Spatial movement pans each complete voice at a different rate. The default uses the same harmonic levels, motion depths, and cycles as your combined audition, running continuously. This is an experimental browser instrument, not the installed Legio firmware. A gentle output compressor catches peaks.

To hear it through an Expert Sleepers ES-8, connect the ES-8 to your computer, choose it as the audio device, then select an output pair. If the browser offers only 1 + 2, use those jacks; some browsers expose only stereo output. Some browsers request microphone access to list named devices; the temporary stream is stopped immediately and never enters the synth.

Current hardware: Ceiling = pitch; Room = detuning; Color turn = octave variation; press + turn = chord; Tilt = original / saw / square; Cut = octave; Color CV = waveform morph. The controls here explore a future bloom engine, so they are intentionally more extensive.
